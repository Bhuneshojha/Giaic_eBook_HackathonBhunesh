# Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics: Academic Book Outline

## Overview
This academic book targets computer science students with a focus on Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics. It covers ROS 2, digital twin simulation, NVIDIA Isaac AI integration, voice-to-action pipelines, and a comprehensive capstone project. The content is designed with Flesch-Kincaid grade level 10-12 and includes academic rigor with proper citations and reproducible examples.
